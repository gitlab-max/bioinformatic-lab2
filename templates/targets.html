Target Identification Workflow

  1. Upload a list of candidate genes (from differential expression or literature).
  2. Map to protein-protein interaction (PPI) networks.
  3. Identify hubs and key modules using network centrality.
  4. Integrate pathway enrichment and functional annotation.
  5. Rank candidates by drugability and disease relevance.
  6. Deliver a prioritized list of potential therapeutic targets (CSV + plots).
